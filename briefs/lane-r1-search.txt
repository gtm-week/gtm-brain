## Lane R1: search that answers in one call
Today: search matches words literally, so "invoice" does not find "invoicing", a typo finds nothing, and a paraphrase finds nothing ("sign-ups", "presenting" and "Thursday night" returned no rows on the real brain); results are not ranked for the question and are not capped (one search returned 60 KB; a blind reader spent 118 KB on one answer); Codex runs two to three searches plus a topic load per question.
Build, inside the existing SQLite FTS index and the query tool (no new dependency, no embeddings, no second store):
- Stemmed matching (FTS5 porter or an equivalent built with Node built-ins) and tolerance for one typo in a word of five letters or more; names and aliases resolve through the entity registry first, so a first name, a nickname recorded as an alias, or a company named by its product finds the entity's notes.
- Filter before text: when the query names an entity, a kind, a tag or a period ("last month", "in October", a date), narrow by those indexed columns, then rank the text.
- Ranking: best matches first (current over superseded, exact phrase over scattered words, newer over older on ties), and never a retracted note.
- A size cap on one reply (default about 12 KB, a setting) with a last line saying how many more matched and how to get them; each hit shows enough of the note to answer from (the note text, its date and age, its id, its source), so a topic load is not needed afterwards.
- One call for a question: a mode of the existing query tool that takes the question as asked and returns, in one reply under the cap, the best notes, the matching entity's short card (kind, the few schema fields, current relations) and their ages, plus "not found" stated plainly when nothing matches (so the model does not keep searching: a negative answer must be cheap). Keep the tool name and existing arguments stable; lib.RESERVED_TOOLS is held equal to the catalog by a test.
- Update the tool description so the model knows one call is enough and when a second is warranted.
Close the e2e known gaps search.stemmed and search.typo. Real-brain numbers to report: for 12 question-like queries of your choosing across people, companies, dates and inflected forms, the rank of the right note, reply bytes and ms, before and after; and the three terms above.

Design choices already settled for this round (apply the ones for your lane):
