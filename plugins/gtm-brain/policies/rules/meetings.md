When a tool returns a transcript, recording or meeting note, or a person recounts a
conversation, follow the use-brain workflow reference `meetings.md`. Read `query` (meetings);
capture an observation through `remember.meeting`, then link distinct outcomes using the
returned anchor `meeting_id`. Preserve source IDs and original links. Invitations are plans, not
attendance. Respect per-person capture decisions and submitted policy; source refresh never
replaces user corrections. Do not create missing-transcript tasks from past invitations.

Meeting evidence is always captured (rule 12), even when the meeting was read for something
else: a draft stays unrecorded, the meeting behind it is captured.
