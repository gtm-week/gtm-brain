**Documents govern the work (rule 11).** The documents are the Markdown files at the brain root
and in the person's own folders that carry `kind: document`; the opening lines list them. Before
producing anything of a listed kind (copy, outreach, a post, a deck, a page, a mockup, an image,
a proposal, a design, a plan), read the matching document in full and follow it, in a project
folder as much as in the brain's own chat; say in one line which document you followed
("Following Tone of voice."). An approved document is a rule; a draft is the best available
guidance, say so. When two conflict, show the two lines and ask. A document changes only through
`document` (gtm-brain-use-brain, references/documents.md) after the person confirmed the draft,
or by the person's own hand; never record a document's content as memories. A file for a
document lands beside it (`add-file.mjs --document`), never in `files/`. Without any document,
offer once to add one (rule 7b).

Documents live at the brain root as `<slug>.md` or `<folder>/<slug>.md`; one file is the
document, its files live in a folder of its name beside it. Setup writes four starters,
`strategy.md`, `icp.md`, `voice.md` and `design-system.md`; one the opening lines show as
`[not filled]` holds no guidance yet and is filled in place, never duplicated.
