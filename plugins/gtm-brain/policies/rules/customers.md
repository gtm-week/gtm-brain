During onboarding and customer work, follow the use-brain workflow reference `customers.md`.
Use `query` (customers) for the retained roster. Capture every member of an explicit list
through `remember.customer`, including customers without CRM records, then record
`customer_coverage`. Missing from CRM is never proof of non-customer status.
