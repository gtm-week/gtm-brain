# Using ZYNT well: the method

By ZYNT (Wojciech Ozimek). How a sales or marketing person gets value from ZYNT every week,
in the words of ZYNT's signals workshop (19 Nov 2026).

## 1. News and signals are two things

News is what ZYNT found about a company or person: an article, a LinkedIn post, a job ad, a
tender. It shows as soon as ZYNT finds it. A signal is news that gives you a reason to talk to
a company now. A company in the news is not a signal yet.

- A timing signal: something happened, so a good time to talk starts. A new decision-maker, a
  funding round, a new market or office, an acquisition.
- A buying signal: the company says it wants to buy. A tender, a request for a vendor,
  "alternative to [tool]", a post about a problem you solve.

A new workspace has news before it has signals: ZYNT first learns what you sell, then picks
the news that matters. Show the news meanwhile, as news.

## 2. A signal has four parts

What happened (one fact, with a source), when it happened and when ZYNT found it, why it
matters to what you sell, and who to contact (a real person: the one in the news, or the one
who decides). Without all four it is news. The score says how relevant it is to you, not how
likely they are to buy.

## 3. Read it against what you sell

The same news means different things to different sellers. Say why it matters to this seller,
from their strategy and ideal customer, and keep ZYNT's score apart from your own judgment.
Say what the source reports; do not add doubts it does not raise.

## 4. Who to contact

One signal, more than one person: the decision maker, one influencer who has the problem, and
a warm introduction if someone on the team knows them. Then give the signal to one person on
the team, so somebody acts on it.

## Output

- The signals first, best first, each in four parts with its link.
- Then the news, newest first, each with its date and link, called news.
- One next step: a person to contact, a company to add, or a list to look at.
