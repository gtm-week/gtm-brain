# Audience discovery method

## Independent roles

An ICP candidate needs current professional evidence of organisation fit and buyer role for
the selected offering. Assess topic fit, organisation fit, buyer role, geography and observed
need separately. Unknown need is not verified need, and a different first job alone does not
establish stale identity. Keep each profile's qualification independent.

A conversation host authors relevant discussions with observed participation by the selected
ICP across distinct relevant posts. One promising thread supports a provisional host, not
confirmed recurring access to the audience. A host can live outside the buyer geography;
verify participant geography and professional fit separately. Followers and reach are not
host qualification. A person may hold both roles, with different evidence and admission outcomes.

## Opportunity quality

Verify the original author and event date, source wording, actionable age and professional
context. A useful contribution adds experience, a specific question, a correction or a distinct
perspective. Agreement alone is weak. Recency of retrieval does not establish discussion recency.
For an older post, dated recent discussion must demonstrate that it remains actionable.

Use the company's freshness and quality targets. If none exist, propose them when they change
the task rather than inheriting another company's numeric defaults. Broaden discovery where
category gaps remain; never fill a quota with weaker or misclassified candidates.

## Returning to a conversation

Recall prior conversation decisions for this profile and canonical thread from the existing feedback store.
Reuse the existing entity and source link. If the founder reports posting, preserve their
exact final wording when supplied and attribute the action to their report. A positive rating,
a draft, and an observed reply are separate facts. For a reply, identify its author, date,
parent and what it adds. A like, silence or an unrelated comment creates no follow-up.
Respect a skip for its stated scope. Surface a previously shown thread again only when a
verified change supplies a new opening, or the founder explicitly asks to revisit it.

Persist shown recommendations as sourced observations in the designated sourced feedback store, including selected
profile, canonical thread URL, branch/comment reference, review time and the proposed opening.
This is what the next session queries before suggesting the same thread. Reuse existing records and stable entity IDs rather than creating a second identity store. Keep these observations distinct from
feedback, user-reported posting and observed replies. Record feedback with that same profile
and thread reference plus the exact response and any stated scope (this thread, this person,
this topic, or this session). "Not today" does not exclude a person or topic. A thread skip does
not reject its author as an ICP. Unclear scope stays local to the shown recommendation.

Judge usefulness by the founder's feedback and sourced substantive exchanges: a question
answered, a reciprocal reply or an agreed next step. Reactions and impressions remain separate
observations. Do not claim relationship progress from activity volume or collect more data
just to fill a scorecard.

Ask for one decision on the conversation when feedback is needed. Save the actual response
and its source in the designated sourced feedback store. Ask for list decisions only when list review is in scope.

## Feedback and retention

Keep source evidence, inference, nomination, approval and actual list change distinct. Record
who gave feedback, on what entity/role/profile, when, and which source message or chat supports
it. Opposing signals and incomplete reads stay holds. Absence of feedback means no decision.
An absent exclusion is only an exclusion-check result, not outbound eligibility. Eligibility
requires the workflow's affirmative prerequisites, including identity, relationship and prior
outreach checks where applicable. Report unverified prerequisites as unknown or hold; a
research shortlist does not admit anyone to outreach.

Before another run, reconcile unresolved collection and delivery attempts and reuse confirmed
results. Preserve rejection reasons and duplicate identities. Store only durable evidence needed
for decisions; apply company retention rules to temporary cache. Report unmet targets honestly.
