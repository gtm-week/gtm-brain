# Starting contexts

Setup creates five shared contexts and the identified creator's personal context. Installing
the plugin alone creates no contexts. Explain this set together; no separate rule interview.

| Context | Stable ID | What belongs here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Our company | `company` | Our organization's identity, mission, team, internal ways of working and onboarding. The workplace info identifies whose brain this is. |
| Strategy | `strategy` | Our priorities, revenue goals, growth choices and go-to-market plan. |
| Market | `market` | Market trends, competitors, alternatives and external research. |
| Target customers | `icp` | Who we sell to, buyer needs, buying roles and evidence of customer fit. |
| Our offer | `offer` | Our products and services, positioning, packaging, pricing and proof. |
| Personal | `personal-<handle>` | This person's role, language and working preferences. |

Our company is not a list of companies. Customers, prospects, competitors and partners are
named references attached to memories with `remember.companies`, visible under Entities.
Choose a context by what the finding means, not because its subject is a company. For example,
in Reflow's brain: Reflow's team change belongs in Our company; Castorama's buying needs in
Target customers; a competitor's pricing in Market; Reflow's pricing in Our offer. A strategic
decision informed by any of those belongs in Strategy, with its evidence linked.

Use an existing sales or account context when its purpose and standing rule fit better. A mention, imported CRM
record or one-off task does not create a context. Propose a new one only for sustained work
with a distinct purpose, and wait for agreement. Do not add pipeline or marketing by default.

Use the displayed names in conversation, translated into the person's language, and the stable
IDs in tool calls. Older brains keep their contexts, custom titles, rules and memories. Do not
create `our-company`, `target-customers` or `our-offer` alongside the existing IDs to rename them.
