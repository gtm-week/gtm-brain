# ZYNT setup: the method

How a ZYNT workspace is set up well, from what a company already knows. From ZYNT's v0.2 test
(Wojciech Ozimek and Filip, 13 Nov 2026): a clean Codex, a sample brain and about 15 minutes from
brain to a live workspace.

## 1. Start from one segment

ZYNT works best with one segment first: one kind of company, in one market, with one reason to
buy. When the ICP names several, start with the one the strategy puts first, and say the others
can follow. When it names none, ask which one to start with.

## 2. Signal types come from the business

A signal is news that gives you a reason to talk to a company now; a company in the news is not
a signal yet. Derive the signal types from what the company sells
and why its customers bought, not from a generic list. For an office-supply dealer: offices that
move, firms that hire, tenders for office supplies, customers unhappy with a competitor. Two to
five types; each one says what it would look like in news, posts or job ads.

## 3. Guesses stay guesses

An assumption, a hypothesis or a draft is sent as what it is, never as a confirmed fact. ZYNT
turns it into a guess the person confirms on the summary. A wrong signal type costs weeks of
noise; checking a guess costs one look.

## 4. Lists of real companies and people

The first lists hold the target companies and people the company already names, not ones guessed
to fit: one list of companies, one of people, since a ZYNT list holds one kind. ZYNT shows each company's news (articles, posts, job ads) at once, so the list is useful
before the first signal.

## 5. The first question

Ask about the list right away: "Which companies on my list had news this week?". ZYNT reads the
last 7 days first, then 30, then 90.

## Output

- One segment, two to five signal types, guesses marked as guesses.
- One list of named target companies, one of named target people.
- The person's "Looks right" in ZYNT.
