# Build a landing page: the method

By Brian Woloszczak and Peakout. The full procedure ships unchanged inside the company's
website project at `docs/workflows/build-landing-page/SKILL.md`, with the SEO manual in
`docs/seo.md`. Read it there. This page names what good looks like.

## 1. Brief first

Audience, campaign, offer, one call to action, the route, and the copy that is approved. The
brief is reviewed before any page is composed.

## 2. Map the brief to sections

Each part of the brief maps to an existing registered section. A gap becomes a new reusable
section, not a one-off block in the page.

## 3. Compose, then SEO

The page is composed from registered sections only, then gets its title, description,
canonical URL and social image.

## 4. Verify

The running page is checked at 375, 768 and 1440 pixels, with its forms and interactions.

## Output

- The page route and its local URL.
- The checks at the three widths.
- Placeholders still in the page and forms with no endpoint, named.
