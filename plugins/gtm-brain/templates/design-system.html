What the numbers mean in use

Surface on Positive5.45. Confirmations and positive tags.

Do and do not

Placeholder. Do: lead with a number, keep one accent colour, leave white space. Do not: gradients, drop shadows, more than two type sizes on a social image. Each pair shows the same content both ways.
