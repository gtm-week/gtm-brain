# Audience and topic research

Start with the audience, then understand its conversations:

| Skill | Main question | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/gtmb-audience-loop` | Who fits our ICP? | Independently assessed buyer candidates |
| `/gtmb-audience-loop` | Where are relevant conversations happening? | Conversation hosts and useful discussion openings with participation evidence |
| `/gtmb-topic-analyzer` | Given our ICP, what conversations are they having? | Sourced themes showing the questions, problems and decisions they discuss, with participation evidence and coverage gaps |

Audience Loop is the natural introduction: identify relevant people and places to listen or
contribute. Topic Analyzer then helps understand what matters to that audience. For example,
given a list of L&D leaders, it can trace their observed posts, comments and engagement to
conversations about training transfer, learning time or AI adoption, and explain who raised
which question and what remains unresolved.

This is an introduction order, not a required execution sequence. Topic Analyzer can start
from an existing ICP list, CRM segment or supplied conversation evidence without an Audience
Loop run. It owns the scoped evidence collection needed for its input list. Interviews and
CRM notes are additional ways to hear that audience. A handoff carries source references,
profile, dates and unresolved questions, never qualification or approval.

Keep the connection to the ICP visible. A reaction shows observed engagement, not agreement;
a relevant host's other posts do not establish that the ICP participated in those conversations.
A broad source sample can suggest themes, but missing audience-participation evidence remains
a gap. Topic priorities follow from the audience's conversations and their relevance to the
selected offering.

Read the selected offering and evidence first. Separate facts, inferred fit, recommendations
and review decisions. Broad topic research can retain material that is unsuitable for a live
contribution. Both skills share the contribution-filtering contract when proposing angles or
conversations. A source link, engagement count or completed step does not prove relevance.

For a short LinkedIn session, check discussion access before setup questions. Lead with one
verified opening or explain the specific evidence gap. Unpublished drafts are not live posts.
Buyer fit and conversation-host qualification require independent evidence; list admission
remains separate. Existing customers or excluded outbound accounts can still inform research
when the exclusion's scope permits it.

## Storage and continuation

Reports use the document tool or an established project destination. Supported findings and
actual feedback use sourced memories in an existing context. Reuse entity IDs and record shown
threads with their profile, branch and review time. Preserve exact corrections and their scope.

Topic analysis and Audience discovery ship as manual draft workflow templates. They use the
existing engine: save a sourced, run-scoped finding or actual review response, verify it, then
advance the matching note milestone. The skill checks the evidence; this change does not add
engine-enforced source validation or new MCP operations. Setup copies the templates to new
brains. Upgrade leaves an existing workflows folder alone; adoption there uses normal workflow
creation. Neither installing nor saving a template activates or schedules it.

## Development and evaluation

Portable methods were developed independently in LinkedIn Agent and are shipped here as
self-contained files. There is no runtime dependency on that repository or Python. Maintainers
can edit these files normally; plugin tests do not require matching an external export receipt.

The [evaluation guide](../plugins/gtm-brain/test/research/README.md) contains the offline checks,
pinned Berlin mock cases, optional provider-snapshot replay, review rubric and limitations.
The public fixture tests source-based decisions and save/resume behavior; it has no live
LinkedIn discussions. Reusable prompts and tests ship here. Personal data, detailed run reports
and collection audits stay in the contributor's local development workspace. No paid collector
or autonomous source acquisition is introduced.

For saved Up2Data envelopes, a bundled local Node reader separates statement speakers from
profile claims, preserves publication and collection dates, verifies exact quotations and
checks contribution gates against the actual policy. The skill classifies the original's
full format before ranking topic fit, including mixed event/reflection and promotion/discussion
posts. Source checks do not automatically establish relevance, a useful angle or admission.

Provider access, budgets, tracking rules, delivery and retention belong to each company.
No collector, paid allowance, delivery destination, global installation or external engagement
is introduced by these skills.

## Connecting a research source

Both skills route missing Up2Data access to the shared
[onboarding procedure](../plugins/gtm-brain/skills/gtmb-topic-analyzer/references/up2data-onboarding.md).
It offers supplied evidence or connection, user-owned signup and credential entry, a sanitized
free account check, then separately scoped spending approval. The included Node helper only
checks access; a suitable collector and its request ledger are still required for paid work.
No private project's credential location, historical balance or spending allowance is inherited.

Provider alternatives are follow-up work: [Apify](https://apify.com), [Needle](https://needle.app),
and [stickerdaniel/linkedin-mcp-server](https://github.com/stickerdaniel/linkedin-mcp-server).
For each, review actual authentication, supported reads, pricing, source coverage and retry
semantics; distinguish read access from any write-capable tools. Add separate onboarding and
bounded provider tests before describing an alternative as supported. The research method and
supplied-file route should remain usable regardless of provider choice.
